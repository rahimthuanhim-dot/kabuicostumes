Kabui Costume Rental – Cultural Use & Disclaimer

Kabui Costume Rental is committed to preserving the cultural identity, heritage, and dignity of the Kabui (Rongmei) community. We kindly request all customers to read and follow the guidelines below before renting our traditional attire.

  1. 1. No Unauthorized Religious Use

    Our Kabui traditional costumes are not to be used in religious dances, rituals, ceremonies, or religious performances without prior approval from Kabui Costume Rental.

  2. 2. Prior Approval Is Mandatory

    Customers must clearly inform us of the intended purpose of the costume at the time of booking. Any use for stage performances, cultural programmes, dance performances, video shoots, promotional activities, or other public events requires prior approval.

  3. 3. Respect for Cultural Identity

    Kabui traditional attire represents our unique cultural heritage and identity. It must not be misrepresented, altered, or used in a manner that disrespects our traditions, customs, or cultural values.

  4. 4. No Unauthorized Representation

    Customers are not permitted to present our costumes as another tribe’s traditional attire or combine them with other cultural elements in a way that misrepresents Kabui culture.

  5. 5. Restricted Use and Violation

    Any use of our costumes beyond the approved purpose, or any unauthorized use that violates these guidelines, may result in additional charges or a penalty, depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation.

  6. 6. Customer Responsibility

    Customers are responsible for informing performers, organizers, photographers, and other participants about the approved use and these conditions.

  7. 7. Right to Refuse Rental

    Kabui Costume Rental reserves the right to refuse or cancel a booking if the intended use does not align with our cultural guidelines.

  8. 8. Agreement to Terms

    By booking or renting our costumes, customers acknowledge and agree to follow these conditions. Please seek clarification and obtain written approval before using the attire for any purpose beyond ordinary personal wear.

We believe our traditional attire deserves respect, understanding, and responsible representation. These guidelines are intended to protect our cultural heritage and ensure that Kabui traditions are treated with dignity.

Thank you for your cooperation and respect for Kabui (Rongmei) culture.

— Kabui Costume Rental, Imphal
